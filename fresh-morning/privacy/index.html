Fresh Morning — Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Fresh Morning: Alcohol Tracker (“the app”) is made by Small Wins Labs (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never see your data. The ads in the app come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data, described in section 3.

1. What you enter stays on your device

Your daily check-ins (No drinks, Moderate, A lot), the amounts you spent, your usual weekly spend, currency, reminder time, language, earned badges, the goals you add (a name and a price) and the date you installed the app are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. We do not receive, see, share or sell this data.

2. Android backup

Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account, so that you can restore your history on a new phone. The backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account settings and Google’s privacy policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data and shares it with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to AdMob. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, which also turns off personalized ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Where privacy laws require it, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before it requests any ads, and personalized ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

4. Purchases

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase.

5. Reminders

The evening reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere to show it.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all the data the app stores on your device. To delete the copy in your Android backup, manage your backups in your Google account.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
